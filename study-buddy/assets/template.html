The idea

Training is learning; inference is using what was learned. Training is one huge job; inference is many small, fast requests.

Exam trap

"Add more GPUs" is wrong when the scenario says latency per request is the problem: that is an inference optimisation question (TensorRT, batching), not a scale question.

DevOps lens · from your world

Training is the nightly full build on the whole agent farm; inference is production traffic on the app servers. Where it breaks: a build finishes, but a model is retrained repeatedly as data changes.

Worked example · memory sizing

A 13B model in FP16 needs 13 × 2 = 26 GB for weights alone, so it fits on one 80 GB GPU for inference with room for the KV cache, but training it (≈13 × 16 = 208 GB) needs at least three such GPUs.
